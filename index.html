Who/What We Are

Astrophage is a small specialty roaster built around one idea: light roasts, tasted clearly. We buy carefully, roast gently, and tell you exactly what went into every bag. Light roast can be a new experience for many people, so we suggest approaching it more like a fruit juice than the medium or dark roasts you'd get from St*rb*cks. Coffee starts out as a fruit, the coffee cherry, so it only feels right to preserve its fruity, sweet, and bright flavors. We encourage you to try it with an open mind and an open palate. Enjoy!
