About Us

Roast

Our coffees range from Nordic Light to Ultralight. We will source two to three exceptional coffees that will adhere to our profile styles and sourcing ideals. We choose one to roast with under the principle of having whimsical fun, while also learning something about roasting.

Our approach is based on a 4kg batch size on a 10 kilo capacity roaster. We charge at 405-415°F on this machine, and keep our airflow relatively open for most of the roast. Our goal is to roast faster in order to preserve more structure in the coffee profile but not too fast as to taste raw or lose solubility. We open airflow and drop gas harshly coming into first crack (we crash our roast curves on purpose). If youre interested in nerding out about this DM me.

Story

Opening a specialty roastery has always been a dream of mine. After a while in the coffee world, becoming friends with so many roasters is what really solidified it for me.

Moving to San Francisco opened a lot of new doors. I got to cup funky, rare coffees alongside a bunch of really cool people, and it made me care about tasting what a coffee actually is, not what the roast turned it into.

Astrophage is where all of that comes together: A big dream in a small cup of coffee(12g doses)

Philosophy

At our core, we're driven by coffee science and curating shared coffee experiences. We tailor roast experiments and explore our curiosities all while being hyper-transparent about our process. It's a project fueled by passion, but also honesty.

Often times it is expected that the producer provides all of their possible info, but rarely the roaster. We are doing our best to flip that script. We value traceability, but ask less when we buy and share more than we probably should. We can do this because we have trust in green quality from our exporter and importer partners. As we grow, we hope to pass on that trust to our customers, while also breaking norms in our whimsical and hyper-honest natures.
